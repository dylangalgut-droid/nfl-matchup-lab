Definitions & coverage

Includes passes with an identified intended receiver in verified final games across the selected season, including playoffs. Depth is air yards to the target. Receiving yards include the catch and yards gained afterwards. Left, middle and right describe the destination of a throw. The feeds used here do not identify where the receiver lined up, so a throw over the middle cannot establish a slot reception.

Each defensive difference uses the league average for that throw type. Per-game figures include games with zero catches of that type. Offensive shares include incompletions and interceptions with an identified target. Screen, play-action and RPO can overlap. Their shares use only targets with all three charting flags available. A dash indicates insufficient data.
